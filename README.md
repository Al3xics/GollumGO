## Game Pitch

**GollumGO** is a tactical board-style puzzle game where you play as the famous tortured creature from Tolkien’s universe.  
Armed with your *precious ring*, you must escape the Nazgûl and the Orcs hunting you through the **Dead Marshes**.

To survive, you’ll need to be clever.  
The Ring allows you to vanish from the eyes of Orcs, but wearing it can expose you to the servants of the Eye.  
Worse still, the Ring only obeys its true master — misuse it, and it may slip from your control.

## Core Concept

Inspired by Tolkien’s universe and Peter Jackson’s trilogy, **GollumGO** is a **tactical puzzle game** built around the unique powers — and dangers — of the One Ring.

The Ring grants invisibility against Orcs, while making you vulnerable to Nazgûl.  
Gameplay revolves around **alternating between wearing and removing the Ring**, carefully planning your moves to reach the exit of each level.

Every decision matters: timing, positioning, and foresight are the keys to survival.

<br>

## IP DNA & Artistic Direction

GollumGO is a **stealth-focused game inspired by The Lord of the Rings universe**, featuring a Gollum-like protagonist.  
The artistic direction is **dark, oppressive, and unsettling**, perfectly aligned with the tone of Middle-earth.

Gollum is not a fighter — he survives through **cunning, hatred, and deception**.  
The Dead Marshes, with their disturbing atmosphere, heighten the tension already created by stealth and strategic thinking.

And of course, Gollum would be nothing without his *precious Ring*.  
Its power is the reason he is relentlessly hunted by the servants of Sauron across the marshes.

For us, this universe naturally lends itself to **strong, meaningful gameplay mechanics**, built around risk, temptation, and survival.
